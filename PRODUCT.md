# PRODUCT.md — Exclusive Pest Solutions

register: brand

## Product Purpose
Premium residential pest control across the Phoenix Valley, AZ. Owner-operated by David Kaiser,
Licensed QA Applicator, AZ Dept. of Agriculture License #10150. Bark scorpion specialists.
The positioning is deliberately anti-volume: "we charge a premium because we're responsible with
the products", slower and more methodical work, wildlife-conscious (raptor-safe rodenticides,
mechanical traps preferred), IPM-certified. 10+ years, 95% first-visit resolution, 5.0 on Google.
The homepage has one job: get a custom quote request from a homeowner who wants the careful option.

## Users
Primary: Phoenix Valley homeowners 35-60 in newer suburban developments (Gold Canyon, Queen Creek,
San Tan, Mesa) who own the home, care about pets/kids/garden, and are willing to pay more for
someone who does it properly. They are not price shopping; they are competence shopping.
Secondary: homeowners in acute scorpion panic who need someone tonight.

Scene sentence: a Phoenix homeowner, 45, on a tablet at 9:40pm having just found a bark scorpion
on the bathroom tile, wants to know who to call and that this person actually knows what they
are doing.
=> Dark theme. Bark scorpions are nocturnal, treatments happen at dusk, and the brand mark is a
   nocturnal raptor. The dark is the subject matter, not a style preference.

## Brand voice
Technical, plain, unhurried, quietly confident. Speaks in specifics: entry points, harborage,
pest biology, label compliance, license numbers. Never salesy, never alarmist, never cute
despite the cartoon owl. The owl is the ethical argument made visual: rodenticides that kill
rodents also kill the raptors that eat them, so Exclusive doesn't use them.

Three physical-object words: engineered, nocturnal, deliberate. Like a licensed applicator's
field manual, or a pesticide label, or desert highway signage lit by headlights.

## Anti-references
- The current site: Tailwind CDN in production, Inter + Manrope, rounded cards with soft shadows,
  soft light gradients. Reads like every other service-business template.
- National pest brands (Terminix, Orkin, Aptive): loud green-and-white, cartoon bug mascots,
  urgency banners, "FREE INSPECTION" everywhere. Exclusive's entire pitch is being the opposite
  of a volume franchise.
- Clean-clinical light-blue "trust" palettes. That is the first reflex for the category.

## Strategic principles
1. One dominant action: GET MY CUSTOM PLAN. Phone is the secondary path for the 9pm panic case.
2. Credentials are the product. License #10150, QA, IPM, Dept. of Ag are load-bearing, not badges.
3. The owl argument (raptor-safe) is the single most differentiating idea on the page. Give it room.
4. Scorpions are the wedge. "Our specialty" on bark scorpions is the reason people search.
5. Real reviews as real text. There are 16 Google reviews currently trapped inside PNG screenshots.
