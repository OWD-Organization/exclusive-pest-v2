1. Overview
Exclusive Pest Solutions ("
Exclusive Pest Solutions
," "
we
," "
us
," or "
our
") is a licensed pest management company serving residential and commercial properties throughout the Phoenix Valley, Arizona. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. It applies to our website, our online forms, phone calls and text messages with our team, and the pest control services we perform at your property.
By using our website or requesting our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use our website or submit information to us.
2. Information We Collect
We collect only the information we need to quote, schedule, perform, and support your pest control service. The categories below describe what we may collect.
Information you give us directly
Contact details
— your name, service address, mailing address, phone number, and email address.
Property and service details
— the type of property, square footage, landscaping and irrigation notes, pest history, gate codes or access instructions, and photos you send us of pest activity.
Household and safety information you choose to share
— the presence of children, pets, beehives, fish or reptile tanks, edible gardens, and any allergies, chemical sensitivities, or health conditions you disclose so we can select appropriate treatment methods.
Scheduling and communication records
— appointment requests, service preferences, notes from calls or texts, and correspondence with our office or technicians.
Payment information
— billing name and address, and the last four digits and expiration date of a card kept on file. Full card and bank account numbers are collected and stored by our third-party payment processor, not by us.
Marketing preferences
— email newsletter signups, review submissions, and referral information.
Information collected automatically
Device and usage data
— IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, referring website, pages viewed, links clicked, and the dates and times of your visit.
Cookies and similar technologies
— small files and identifiers placed on your device, as described in Section 6.
Approximate location
— a general geographic area (typically city or ZIP code level) derived from your IP address, used to confirm you are within our service area.
Information from other sources
Referral sources
— your name and contact information when a neighbor, property manager, real estate agent, or existing customer refers you to us.
Lead and advertising platforms
— inquiries submitted through directories, search engines, social media, or review sites.
Publicly available records
— property information used to prepare an accurate estimate.
We do not intentionally collect Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, or other sensitive personal information from customers, and we ask that you not send such information to us.
3. How We Collect It
We collect information when you:
Complete a quote, contact, or appointment request form on our website;
Call, text, or email us, or respond to a message from us;
Schedule, receive, or pay for a pest control service;
Subscribe to our seasonal pest alerts or email newsletter;
Interact with our advertisements, social media profiles, or review listings; or
Browse our website, where cookies and analytics tools operate automatically.
4. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
Prepare estimates and respond to your questions and service requests;
Schedule, route, and perform pest control treatments at your property;
Select treatment materials and methods appropriate for your household, pets, pollinators, and landscaping;
Maintain service records, application logs, and other documentation required by Arizona pest management regulations;
Send appointment confirmations, technician arrival notices, service reports, follow-up reminders, and warranty or re-service notifications;
Process payments, issue invoices and receipts, and collect amounts owed;
Provide customer support and resolve complaints or service concerns;
Send seasonal pest alerts, promotions, and other marketing communications where permitted, and where you have not opted out;
Analyze website traffic and service demand to improve our website, our offerings, and our advertising;
Detect and prevent fraud, protect our property and personnel, and enforce our agreements; and
Comply with applicable federal, Arizona, and local laws, regulations, licensing requirements, subpoenas, and lawful requests.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.
5. Phone Calls & Text Messages
When you provide your phone number, you agree that we may contact you at that number by phone call and text message regarding estimates, scheduling, technician arrival, service results, billing, and related account matters. These are transactional messages tied to the service you requested.
If you separately opt in to promotional or marketing text messages, you agree that we may send those messages using an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded voice where applicable. Consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.
Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
Message frequency varies based on your service schedule and preferences.
Reply
STOP
to any text message to opt out, or
HELP
for assistance. You may also call or email us using the details in Section 16.
Opting out of marketing messages does not stop transactional messages necessary to deliver a service you have scheduled.
Calls to and from our office may be recorded or monitored for quality assurance and training purposes. Where recording occurs, we will disclose it at the start of the call.
6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
Our website uses cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies for the following purposes:
Strictly necessary
— to load pages, remember form entries, and keep the site secure and functional.
Analytics
— to understand how visitors find and use our site, which pages are most useful, and where the experience can be improved. This may include services such as Google Analytics.
Advertising and conversion measurement
— to measure the performance of our advertising and, in some cases, to show our ads to visitors who have previously visited our website. This may include tools provided by Google, Meta, or similar advertising platforms.
Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of our website function. You can also opt out of certain interest-based advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance at
optout.aboutads.info
or the Network Advertising Initiative at
optout.networkadvertising.org
.
Do Not Track.
Because no common industry standard for responding to browser "Do Not Track" signals has been adopted, our website does not currently respond to those signals.
7. How We Share Information
We share personal information only as described below. We do not sell it.
Service providers.
Companies that operate on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations, including website hosting, customer relationship and scheduling software, routing and dispatch tools, email and text messaging platforms, payment processors, accounting services, and analytics and advertising vendors.
Our technicians and staff.
Employees and licensed applicators who need your service address, access instructions, and safety notes to perform your treatment.
Property owners, managers, or HOAs.
Where service is arranged or paid for by a landlord, property manager, or homeowners association, we may share service dates, treatment records, and related documentation with that party.
Regulators.
The Arizona Department of Agriculture and other agencies with jurisdiction over pest management, where records, pesticide application information, or complaint responses are required by law.
Legal and safety.
Where we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or government request; to enforce our Terms of Service or other agreements; to collect amounts owed; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our customers, our personnel, or the public.
Business transfers.
In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business, customer information may be transferred as a business asset, subject to this Policy.
With your direction.
Any other disclosure you request or authorize, such as sharing a service report with a real estate agent or home inspector.
8. Data Retention
We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy. Service and pesticide application records are retained for at least the period required by Arizona pest management regulations and applicable federal law. Billing and tax records are retained for the periods required by accounting and tax rules. Marketing contact information is retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or render it de-identified.
9. Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access or disclosure. These include encrypted website connections, access controls limiting employee access to what each role requires, and vetted third-party processors for payment handling.
No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach of unencrypted, unredacted personal information occurs, we will notify affected individuals as required by Arizona's data breach notification law (A.R.S. § 18-552) and any other applicable law.
10. Your Choices & Rights
Regardless of where you live, you may:
Access or correct
the contact and service information we hold about you by calling or emailing us;
Unsubscribe from marketing email
by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing message;
Stop marketing texts
by replying STOP;
Ask us to delete
information that is not required for an active service agreement, a warranty obligation, a regulatory record, or a legal or accounting requirement;
Ask us not to call
your number for marketing purposes; and
Withdraw consent
you previously gave, on a going-forward basis.
To exercise any of these choices, contact us using the details in Section 16. We may need to verify your identity and your connection to the service address before acting on a request. We will respond within a reasonable period and will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy choices.
11. Arizona Residents
Arizona has not enacted a comprehensive consumer data privacy statute. Even so, we extend the choices described in Section 10 to all Arizona customers as a matter of practice.
Arizona law does govern how we handle security incidents and how we conduct business generally. We comply with Arizona's data breach notification requirements (A.R.S. § 18-552) and the Arizona Consumer Fraud Act (A.R.S. § 44-1521
et seq.
). Records relating to pesticide applications performed at your property are maintained in accordance with the requirements of the Arizona Department of Agriculture, which regulates pest management businesses in this state, and may be produced to that agency on request or in response to a complaint.
12. Residents of Other States
Our services are performed in Arizona, but our website may be viewed from anywhere. If you are a resident of a state with a comprehensive consumer privacy law — such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, or Texas — you may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to obtain a copy, the right to correction or deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising.
We do not sell personal information and do not knowingly process sensitive personal information for profiling. To submit a request under any applicable state privacy law, contact us using the details in Section 16 and identify the state in which you reside. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.
13. Children's Privacy
Our website and services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
14. Third-Party Links & Services
Our website may link to third-party sites, including review platforms, social media profiles, mapping services, and payment pages. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit before providing information to it.
15. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices, our technology, or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and post the revised version here. Material changes will be communicated more prominently where required by law. Your continued use of our website or services after an update takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
